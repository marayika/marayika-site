why the name —

Hope is our inheritance.

The name comes from a poem by nikka ursula. It found me when I kept asking myself what I could possibly have done to deserve the pain I was in.

The poem was kind and sad, a soft reminder that what I was feeling was deeply human and universal. It also reminded me that we cannot measure how good or bad we are with how lucky or unlucky we are. There’s so much we can do but sometimes, life just happens. I think knowing the difference helps us give ourselves a little grace, and extend that same grace to others.

Books carried me through those days and allowed me imagine all the beautiful epilogues I could have, and that’s how I have stayed alive to tell this story.

Soft Epilogue exists to pass that hope on.
