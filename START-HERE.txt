MARAYIKA.COM — HOW YOUR WEBSITE WORKS
=====================================

Your site is live at https://marayika.com

Where everything lives
----------------------
- Domain: Canva (marayika.com). Renews yearly in Canva.
- Website files: GitHub, repository marayika/marayika-site (public).
- Hosting: GitHub Pages. Free, no badge.
- Diary editor: Pages CMS, app.pagescms.org (sign in with GitHub).
- Email: hello@marayika.com, through Google. Not affected by the website.

Every change you save on GitHub or in Pages CMS rebuilds the site.
It is live about one minute later.


ADD A DIARY PIECE
-----------------
1. Go to app.pagescms.org and sign in with GitHub.
2. Open marayika-site, then Diary, then "Add an entry".
3. Fill in:
   - Title
   - Link: the Substack (or other) address of the piece. Leave it empty for Gallery work.
   - Subtitle: one line under the title.
   - Date
   - Type: Poetry, Essay or Gallery.
   - Cover image, and a short description of it.
   - Gallery pieces only: up to 3 images and an optional handwritten caption.
4. Click Save.

The newest pieces show first. Numbers and the alternating layout happen on their own.
Saving publishes. Keep unfinished drafts somewhere else.


CHANGE TEXT ON OTHER PAGES
--------------------------
1. Go to github.com/marayika/marayika-site
2. Open src, then the page folder (portfolio, soft-epilogue, contact),
   or src/index.html for Home.
3. Open index.html and click the pencil icon.
4. Change the words between the tags. Leave everything inside < > alone.
5. Click "Commit changes".


DO NOT CHANGE IN CANVA
----------------------
Canva > Settings > Web domains > marayika.com > Advanced settings:
- A records "@": 185.199.108.153, 185.199.109.153, 185.199.110.153, 185.199.111.153
- CNAME "www": marayika.github.io
- MX, SPF and DKIM records: your email. Never delete them.
Keep "Connected to Canva" switched off.
